/*
 * Preserve the approved Georgia family and its native regular/bold/italic faces.
 * Windows Georgia lacks 88 letters in U+1EA0–1EF9; its supported Ỳ/ỳ stay native.
 * Ordinary font fallback decomposes some of them, leaving detached tone marks.
 * A composite face routes ONLY those complete letters to Gelasio, whose metrics
 * match Georgia. Supported glyphs continue to come from the installed Georgia.
 * Display strings must be NFC (normalizeDisplayText); input/storage stay untouched.
 * Gelasio is also the serif substitute on devices where Georgia is unavailable.
 */
